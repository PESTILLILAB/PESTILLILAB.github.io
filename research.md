Our work falls into five primary areas. The first three advance brain understanding: the organization of its long-range axonal connections, the wiring of the visual system, and the changes in white matter that accompany learning or disease. The remaining two concern the means by which such questions can be answered at all: the computational models, data infrastructure, standards, and AI systems that turn large neuroscience datasets into reproducible results, and questions of ethics for AI and data sharing across labs and countries. The papers for each area are listed under [Publications](/publications/).
