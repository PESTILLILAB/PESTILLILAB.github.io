Five areas. Three are about the brain: how its long-range connections are organized, how the visual system is wired, and how white matter changes when people learn. Two are about the means: the computing, standards, and AI that make the measurements usable, and the rules under which brain data can cross borders. Each area gives the question, what we have found, and what comes next; the papers behind each are listed under [Publications](/publications/).
