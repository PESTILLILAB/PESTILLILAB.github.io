Our work falls into five areas. The first three concern the brain itself: the organization of its long-range axonal connections, the wiring of the visual system, and the changes in white matter that accompany learning. The remaining two concern the means by which such questions can be answered at all: the computational infrastructure, data standards, and AI systems that turn large neuroimaging datasets into reproducible results, and the legal frameworks under which brain data collected in one country can be analyzed in another. The papers for each area are listed under [Publications](/publications/).
