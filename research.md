The laboratory pursues two integrated research programs. One investigates how axonal connections and white matter support human perception and cognition across development, learning, aging, and injury. The other develops data science and AI technologies to improve scientific rigor, reproducibility, and access to data across disciplines. Each investigation contributes theoretical understanding alongside reusable data and executable methods, so that other scientists can build on the results. The papers are on the [publications](/publications/) page.
