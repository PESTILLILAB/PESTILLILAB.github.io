brainlife.io connects neuroscience data to computational models and applications, and runs them on cloud systems and high-performance clusters. Every analysis performed on brainlife.io is accompanied by a provenance graph, so anyone can reproduce a result exactly, rerun it with a different model, or apply it to a new population. It handles MRI, diffusion MRI, and MEG/EEG; light-sheet microscopy, polarization-sensitive optical coherence tomography (PS-OCT), and tract tracing are being added, among other modalities.

We started building brainlife.io in 2016. It now runs most of what the lab computes: it hosts the data and processing services for the brain-connectivity consortia below, and its provenance model is where our new work on AI-ready, agent-mediated science starts.
