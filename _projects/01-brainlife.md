brainlife.io connects neuroimaging data to versioned applications, parameters, and outputs in one place, and runs them on cloud systems and high-performance clusters. Every analysis is recorded as a provenance graph, so anyone can reproduce a result exactly, rerun it with a different model, or apply it to a new population. It handles MRI, diffusion MRI, MEG/EEG, and other modalities, and it is used for research and for teaching at institutions that have no computing of their own.

We started building brainlife.io in 2015. It is now the base for most of what the lab does: it hosts the data and processing services for the brain-connectivity consortia below, and its provenance model is where our work on AI-ready, agent-mediated science starts.
