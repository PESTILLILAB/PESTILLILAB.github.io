BRAIN CONNECTS is the NIH BRAIN Initiative program to develop the technology for mapping wiring across the whole brain, from single axons to long-range projections. APEX is the program's data coordination center, which we develop and host. We build the data technologies, standards, and quality metrics for combining connectivity measurements from microscopy, tract tracing, and MRI across species, delivered through services such as [connects.brainlife.io](https://connects.brainlife.io).

The systems have to handle petabyte-scale reconstruction while keeping track of how tissue samples, coordinates, reconstruction methods, and uncertainty relate to one another, so that the anatomy can support reproducible inference as the data grow. We also organize the consortium's annual meetings and hackathons.
