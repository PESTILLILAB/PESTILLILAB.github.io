Global Brain Governance turns the research from BRIDGE into tools that collaborations can use now: a legal map of how jurisdictions regulate neural data, kept up to date; decision tools that classify risk and choose a transfer pathway; a modular agreement for transferring and accessing neurodata; machine-readable governance labels; and a proposed registry of models trained on neural data. The aim is that protections stay in place as data move, are transformed, and are used to train AI.

Partners include the BRIDGE community, the University of Nottingham, ZHAW Zurich, the D'Or Institute, the University of the Witwatersrand, the University of Oxford, McGill University, and the International Brain Initiative.
