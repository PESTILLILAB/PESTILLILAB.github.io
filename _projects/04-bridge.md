BRIDGE is building an international governance framework for brain and mental-health data. It brings together neuroscientists, lawyers, ethicists, regulators, and people with lived experience from Africa, Europe, and the Americas; has assessed the laws of more than ninety countries; and produces legal guides, technology, and a living handbook on international data governance, so that regulation does not stop research.

We have held regional and global workshops in Austin (2024) and Johannesburg (2025). The long-term goal is a self-sustaining global consortium that keeps the framework current.
