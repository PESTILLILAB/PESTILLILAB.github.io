The Center for Mesoscale Connectomics images the same non-human primate brains with tract tracing, polarization-sensitive optical imaging, light-sheet microscopy, and ultra-high-field MRI, starting with the frontoparietal systems that support attention and cognitive control. The goal is a wiring map in which the same axons can be followed at every scale of measurement. That is the ground truth human tractography has been missing.

We lead the center's data informatics: the standards, pipelines, and web services that bring multi-terabyte imaging volumes together with MRI, keep them registered to one another, and release them for anyone to use.
