We led the [BIDS Connectivity project](https://pestillilab.github.io/bids-connectivity), a community extension of the Brain Imaging Data Structure that describes macroscopic brain connections and the workflows that produce them, and we develop [ezBIDS](https://brainlife.io/ezbids), a tool that walks people through converting raw scanner data into the standard so that it works with the major archives and platforms.

Franco Pestilli serves on the BIDS Steering Committee, chairs the Infrastructure Committee of the International Neuroinformatics Coordinating Facility ([INCF](https://incf.org)), and chaired the data-sharing working group of the International Brain Initiative. Earlier infrastructure work includes the Advanced Computational Neuroscience Network and the connectome-mapping algorithms developed under the NSF BRAIN and Big Data programs.
