We study brain connections and how they implement perception, cognition, and learning, in health and disease. We combine multimodal data with computational methods to understand how brain connections are organized and how that organization relates to brain function. In parallel, we develop data-science technology that extends what can be studied with brain data. In the era of artificial intelligence, our ability to understand the brain can be greatly augmented, if it is done responsibly.

*The brain works like the internet: long-range axonal connections link neurons in distant areas, and nothing works without them. This network infrastructure is not fixed. It changes with learning, during development, and as a result of aging and disease.*
