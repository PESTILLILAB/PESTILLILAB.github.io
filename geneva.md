blocks:
  - title: The course
    text: >-
      PSY 355N covers how the brain supports cognition: perception, memory, attention, decision-making,
      and learning. Students work with brain imaging data, neural network models, and the methods of
      experimental design, with an emphasis on reading evidence critically. The lectures are the same as
      in Austin. What changes is everything around them.
  - title: Why Geneva
    text: >-
      Campus Biotech houses one of the densest concentrations of neuroscience and neurotechnology in
      Europe, and the University of Geneva, the University of Lausanne, and EPFL are all within reach.
      Students visit laboratories, meet the scientists whose papers they read in class, and see how
      research is organized in a different country. Franco Pestilli has long-standing collaborations with
      the faculty there.
  - title: Life in the program
    text: >-
      Four weeks of classes, lab visits, and a research project, together with the things that make
      Geneva Geneva: the lake, the mountains, boat trips and hikes, and day trips in Switzerland and
      France. Students live together in apartment hotels or University of Geneva housing. The program
      fee covers housing, some meals and activities, local transport, and orientation; airfare and
      personal expenses are separate, and scholarships are available through Education Abroad.

Every May a group of UT Austin undergraduates spends four weeks studying cognitive neuroscience at the University of Geneva. Franco Pestilli created the program and teaches it. It is open to students from every college at UT.
