We study children learning to read and do math, adults as they age, and people recovering from injury. A scan taken before learning predicts how well a child will learn, and the timing of the changes that follow tells acquisition apart from retention. To see change as it happens, we scan the same people many times within a window where change is fast: a school year, an intervention, a summer break. That lets us follow each person's trajectory instead of averaging it away. The same approach tells us how the state of connections after a concussion predicts who recovers.
