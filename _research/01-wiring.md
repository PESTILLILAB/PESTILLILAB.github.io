Most detailed evidence about brain connections comes from tracer studies in macaques and rodents. In humans, diffusion tractography estimates pathways indirectly, and an estimate can look right while routing a connection through the wrong tissue. We calibrate the human estimates against ground truth: tract tracing and optical microscopy in non-human primate brains that are also imaged with MRI, so the same fibers are seen at every resolution. The result we are after is a validated, population-level wiring diagram of the human brain, with uncertainty attached, usable in models of perception and cognition and in surgical planning.
