For ten years we have operated open cloud infrastructure that links imaging data to versioned applications, parameters, and outputs, so that any analysis can be run again on new data or with a different model. Thousands of researchers and students use it. Around it we work on data standards and community benchmarks. The benchmarks show where methods fail; deep-learning models trained on young adults, for example, do poorly on children. As trained models and AI agents start to do parts of the work, sharing data, code, and methods is no longer enough. Infrastructure has to keep the whole path from measurement to model to claim, so that results produced with AI can be checked.
